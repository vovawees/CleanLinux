cleanlinux-install 0.8.1 (CLI) -- src/main.c plan.c system.c copy.c util.c
- source: the live medium /run/cleanlinux/live/medium: @/ + nonfree/ (default; --no-nonfree: without it and
  ALLOW_NONFREE=0) + spins/NAME/ (--spin NAME), copied as overlayfs would show them (copy.c: owners, modes,
  xattrs, hard links, whiteouts and opaque dirs applied), never live/. --source DIR: another tree (+ nonfree/,
  spins/ next to it). Progress "Copied 45% (1.1 GiB of 2.4 GiB)". No package rebuild, no package cache needed.
- whole DISK: sfdisk GPT (ESP 512 MiB vfat /boot + root btrfs subvolumes @ @home @snapshots, zstd:1) or --target DIR
  pre-mounted (ESP at /boot|/efi|/boot/efi). NO encryption (#14: --encrypt and LUKS removed in 0.7.0).
- config: /etc/kernel/cmdline (+ --cmdline), fstab by PARTUUID, hostname, /etc/localtime (--timezone),
  /etc/vconsole.conf (--keymap --font), burn.conf of the medium (+PROFILE=--profile; then FIRMWARE= lines
  commented out: conf_unset), root pw + user (wheel + audio video render input seat when /etc/group has them), --autologin: /etc/cleanlinux/autologin
  (getty of cleanlinux-boot 0.5.1 logs the user in on tty1; cleanlinux.autologin=0 disables), dinit boot.d:
  dhcpcd, iwd if /sys/class/net/*/wireless (+ /var/lib/iwd/*.psk|open|8021x copied), cleanlinux-boot --root /mnt
  kernel sync, EFI boot entry first in BootOrder (efivars; --no-efi-entry).
- the medium disk is never offered/accepted (0.8.1: btrfs medium found via the mount source, not st_dev).
